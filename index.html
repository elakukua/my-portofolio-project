04 — Data Quality

Every figure passes a checkpoint before it moves on.

This is not a project, it's the principle behind every system above. Data quality is not a step at the end — it is what happens at each handover between systems. Below is what a single figure goes through on its way to a report.

01
Is it defined?
Indicator reference sheets fix the definition, disaggregation and counting rule before collection starts.
02
Is it valid at entry?
Form logic and constraints catch impossible entries while the enumerator is still with the respondent.
03
Did it arrive intact?
Automated transfer removes re-keying — the figure stored is the figure recorded.
04
Is it consistent?
ITT and MTT reconciled in both directions, with deduplication and category harmonisation.
05
Can it be traced?
Every correction carries a logged reason. Error-suppressing formulas removed so failures stay visible.
Grant-funded QA · Reconciliation
A two-directional reconciliation that surfaced a missing reporting period.

Grant Project — MEAL Quality Assurance Review

Full QA review of a grant-funded project, cross-referencing two donor progress reports against the internal tracking dashboard in both directions.

ProblemDonor-facing reports and the internal tracking dashboard did not agree, with no record of which figure was authoritative.
MethodReconciled the Indicator Tracking Table against the Monitoring Tracking Table in both directions, built a cross-reference workbook against both donor reports, and logged every discrepancy with a decision trail.
ResultA missing period of achievements and a set of suspected transcription errors identified, a corrected dashboard delivered, and a QA process other projects can reuse.
