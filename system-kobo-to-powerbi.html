The same route every collection round, so rounds stay comparable.

Field data cleaned differently each cycle cannot be compared across cycles. Fixing the route matters more than any single clean-up.

One fixed route — repeated every collection round

01

Form design

  • Constraints and skip logic so contradictory answers cannot be submitted
  • Choice lists rather than free text wherever a category exists
  • Required fields limited to what is genuinely required — over-forcing produces false answers
  • GPS, photo and timestamp where verification will be needed
02

Enumerator setup

  • Train on the counting rules and the form together, never separately
  • Walk through the edge cases where two enumerators would reasonably disagree
  • Pilot with real respondents, review same-day, correct before scaling
  • Enumerator identifiers so anomalies can be traced and coached
03

Cleaning

  • Structural checks first: expected submissions by site, duplicates, incomplete records
  • Logical checks: internal contradictions, outliers reviewed rather than deleted
  • Harmonisation of units, dates, categories and location names
  • Nothing deleted silently — every correction logged with its reason
04

Analysis

  • Cleaned data reshaped into the flat structure the model expects
  • Measures written once against that structure
  • Disaggregation derived from fields fixed at form design
05

Power BI

  • A new round is a data refresh, not a rebuild
  • Round-on-round comparison, which only works because the route did not change
  • Turnaround from field to dashboard in days rather than weeks
